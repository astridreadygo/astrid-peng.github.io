About

I am a CS PhD student at the University of Utah, working at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, XR, and multimodal systems. I believe the most interesting questions in computing are not about what machines can do, but about how technology changes what humans can think, feel, and express. It is a medium, not a replacement.

I began my HCI research journey as a research assistant at Tsinghua and Aalto. I hold a BS in Mathematics and a BE in Industrial Design from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, graduating with the Excellent Graduate Award.

Research

I design interfaces that treat humans not as users to be optimized, but as minds to be extended in how they think, communicate, and perceive.

2024
Angel vs. Devil

A conversational AI system featuring two opposing agents — one supportive, one adversarial — designed to scaffold critical thinking. Explores how structured cognitive conflict can strengthen reasoning rather than resolve it too quickly.

LLM Agents Critical Thinking HCI
2023
Whispering in XR

A multimodal system for private communication in extended reality environments. Recreates the intimacy and social dynamics of whispering through spatial audio, gaze detection, and proximity sensing — bringing nuanced human communication into digital space.

XR Multimodal Social Computing
ongoing
Attention Stewardship

Exploring how eye tracking can enable ambient AI agents to monitor and manage human attention during complex tasks — allowing people to step away without losing context, and returning them at the right moment.
